## Testing Day

As our project is not a traditional Software Engineering Project, we'll struggle participating in a "user" testing day as we (by the definition of our project) have no users.
Therefore, our team has decided to focus on gathering feedback and data on other aspects of our prototype. This will be in the form of the "users" views on roadside litter,
concerns for safety as a driiver/passenger and just general typed feedback on our prototype to try and gather ideas for features from other bright computer scientists.
